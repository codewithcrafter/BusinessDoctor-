# Marketplace Management & ROAS Scaling: 4.6x Ad Return for Apex Industrial

**Client:** Apex Industrial | **Industry:** Industrial Equipment & B2B Retail  
**Practice Area:** E-Commerce & Marketplace Management | **Verified Impact:** 4.6x ROAS / 94% Buy Box Control (Marketplace Revenue Scale)  
**Canonical Source:** [https://businessdoctor.com/case-studies/multichannel-retail-marketplace-dominance](https://businessdoctor.com/case-studies/multichannel-retail-marketplace-dominance)

## Verified Impact Metrics

- **Baseline Status:** 1.8x ROAS / 34% Buy Box Loss
- **Verified Outcome:** 4.6x ROAS / 94% Buy Box Control
- **Measured Metric:** Marketplace Revenue Scale

## 01 // The Challenge & Business Friction

Apex Industrial was losing Buy Box share on Amazon and Flipkart due to unauthorized resellers, poor product titles, and unoptimized ad campaigns.

## 02 // Engineering Architecture & Solution

BusinessDoctor implemented Brand Registry brand gating, redesigned catalog listings with rich A+ lifestyle modules, and restructured Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Products campaigns.

## 03 // Commercial Results & Quantified Impact

Buy Box possession stabilized at 94%. Blended advertising return climbed from 1.8x to 4.6x while net marketplace operating profit margin expanded by 11.2 percentage points.
