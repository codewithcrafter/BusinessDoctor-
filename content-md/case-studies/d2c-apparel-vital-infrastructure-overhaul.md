# Custom E-Commerce Architecture: Sub-Second Load & 162% Revenue Surge for AURA Atelier

**Client:** AURA Atelier | **Industry:** Luxury D2C & Apparel  
**Practice Area:** Web Development & UI/UX Design | **Verified Impact:** 0.68s Page Load / 3.9% Conv (Net Conversion Surge)  
**Canonical Source:** [https://businessdoctor.com/case-studies/d2c-apparel-vital-infrastructure-overhaul](https://businessdoctor.com/case-studies/d2c-apparel-vital-infrastructure-overhaul)

## Verified Impact Metrics

- **Baseline Status:** 4.8s Page Load / 1.4% Conv
- **Verified Outcome:** 0.68s Page Load / 3.9% Conv
- **Measured Metric:** Net Conversion Surge

## 01 // The Challenge & Business Friction

AURA Atelier suffered heavy cart abandonment on mobile devices due to an outdated, plugin-heavy theme with 4.8-second load times and broken mobile layouts.

## 02 // Engineering Architecture & Solution

We engineered a bespoke, lightweight PHP/HTML5 storefront with dynamic image compression, instant AJAX cart transitions, and a streamlined 1-page checkout flow.

## 03 // Commercial Results & Quantified Impact

Mobile page load dropped to 680 milliseconds. Mobile checkout abandonment declined by 52%, resulting in an immediate $1.85M revenue lift within 60 days of launch.
