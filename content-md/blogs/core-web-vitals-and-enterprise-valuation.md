## Executive Summary

> Page speed is a major ranking factor for Google and a critical driver of user conversions. An engineering breakdown of how clean architecture delivers sub-second load times.

## Article Content

Speed is not a cosmetic feature; it is the fundamental benchmark of web engineering. Google search algorithms prioritize fast, responsive pages that pass Core Web Vitals metrics.

Yet many enterprise sites are weighed down by bloated themes, unmanaged tracking scripts, and heavy frameworks. When a website requires 4.5 seconds to load on mobile, over 40% of visitors abandon the page before interacting.

Building clean, semantic, server-rendered web applications with optimized database caching is one of the most cost-effective investments a business can make.
