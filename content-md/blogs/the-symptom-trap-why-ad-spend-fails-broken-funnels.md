# Why Increasing Ad Spend on a Slow, Broken Website Drains Your Marketing Budget

**Author:** Dr. Harrison Croft (Managing Partner & Chief Solutions Officer)  
**Published:** 2026-11-02 | **Reading Time:** 7 min read | **Category:** Web & Digital Strategy  
**Canonical Source:** [https://businessdoctor.com/insights/the-symptom-trap-why-ad-spend-fails-broken-funnels](https://businessdoctor.com/insights/the-symptom-trap-why-ad-spend-fails-broken-funnels)

## Executive Summary

> When customer acquisition costs rise, marketing agencies often suggest doubling the ad budget. Learn why auditing web performance, mobile speed, and user experience must come first.

## Article Content

In digital commerce, pouring paid traffic into a slow, poorly designed website is like trying to fill a bucket with holes in the bottom. 

If your conversion rate is 1.2% because your website takes 4 seconds to load and your mobile checkout is confusing, doubling ad spend does not double revenue—it simply burns your budget twice as fast.

High-growth businesses begin with technical optimization: achieving 100% Core Web Vitals, removing render-blocking JavaScript, streamlining checkout flows, and aligning value propositions. Once the technical foundation is fast and frictionless, ad spend scales profitably.
