        FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

## Common Questions About Cloud Solutions & IT Consulting

        Everything you need to know about our scope, timeline, engineering standards, communication, and ongoing support.

          Which cloud provider do you recommend: AWS, Google Cloud, or Microsoft Azure?
          +

The ideal provider depends on your existing technology stack, compliance requirements, and business goals. AWS offers the most mature ecosystem of managed services; Google Cloud excels in Kubernetes (GKE), containerization, and data analytics; Microsoft Azure is ideal for enterprise organizations with extensive Active Directory and Microsoft enterprise agreements. We architect with Infrastructure as Code to ensure you are never locked into a single vendor.

          How can you guarantee zero downtime during a major cloud migration?
          +

We utilize blue/green and canary deployment architectures. We provision your entire new cloud infrastructure in parallel, synchronize databases in real time, run automated validation test suites, and then shift DNS traffic over gradually. If any unexpected anomaly is detected, traffic shifts back instantly without any user disruption.

          What is FinOps and how do you reduce our monthly cloud bill?
          +

FinOps is the practice of continuous cloud financial management. We analyze your historical utilization metrics to eliminate zombie volumes, convert on-demand instances to Reserved or Savings Plans, right-size compute resources, deploy auto-scaling, and configure lifecycle rules on storage buckets. This typically yields a 30% to 50% permanent cost reduction.

          Will our internal engineering team be able to maintain the cloud infrastructure?
          +

Yes. Because we author 100% of infrastructure using standard Terraform and Docker code, everything is transparent, version-controlled, and thoroughly documented. We conduct hands-on training sessions with your development leads and provide complete architectural documentation.

          What is the difference between RTO and RPO in disaster recovery?
          +

RTO (Recovery Time Objective) is the maximum acceptable duration of time your systems can be down following a failure. RPO (Recovery Point Objective) is the maximum acceptable age of files or data that must be recovered from backup storage for normal operations to resume. We design architectures with sub-15 minute RTO and sub-5 minute RPO standards.

          How do you handle secrets, API keys, and credentials in the cloud?
          +

We strictly ban hardcoding credentials in application repositories. We implement enterprise secrets management systems such as AWS Secrets Manager or HashiCorp Vault with automated secret rotation, encrypted storage, and least-privilege IAM policy enforcement.

          Can you help us prepare for SOC 2 Type II or HIPAA audits?
          +

Yes. We configure private VPC topologies, enforce end-to-end encryption (AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit), configure centralized immutable log aggregation, implement multi-factor authentication, and provide architectural evidence required by certified compliance auditors.

          Do you provide ongoing 24/7 emergency support after the migration is complete?
          +

Yes. We offer ongoing Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) retainer plans that include 24/7 automated synthetic monitoring, on-call emergency response, proactive security patching, and regular architecture optimization reviews.
