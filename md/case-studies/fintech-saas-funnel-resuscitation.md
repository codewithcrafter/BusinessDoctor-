## 1. Challenge & Operational Bottlenecks
PayGuard was burning $85,000 monthly across Google Ads and LinkedIn without generating qualified enterprise demos. Their website was slow, messaging was unclear, and 88% of inbound traffic dropped off before completing the demo form.

## 2. Prescribed Technical Solution
BusinessDoctor redesigned and rebuilt the core web application using custom PHP/HTML5, built an interactive product demo widget, and restructured their Google Search and LinkedIn paid media campaigns around high-intent keywords.

## 4. Quantifiable Business Outcomes
Within 90 days, demo booking conversions climbed by 318% while customer acquisition costs fell by 65%. Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) grew by $3.4M in two quarters.
